## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, mindful, and reassuring commerce experience for curated pre-loved homeware and furniture. Moving away from cluttered secondhand marketplaces, the aesthetic fuses Scandinavian functional restraint with Japanese wabi-sabi appreciation for aged materials (Japandi) alongside modern sustainable retail standards.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Cared-for & Transparent:** Secondhand items evoke hesitation around hygiene and wear. The interface counters this with pristine typography, rigorous condition grading badges, and high-clarity photography frames.
- **Warmly Grounded:** Oatmeal linen canvases and deep foliage undertones replace sterile digital whites, creating the sensation of walking into a sunlit artisan workshop or living room.
- **Editorial & Timeless:** Clean layout rules and serif headings present each item as a unique historical artifact rather than generic surplus inventory.

### Design Movement
- **Tactile Warm Minimalism:** Generous structural breathing room, organic color temperature, subtle clay and linen borders, warm diffuse ambient lighting, and delicate pill-shaped tagging.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from natural ceramics, aged oak, raw linen, and mossy pine groves. Contrast ratios strictly adhere to WCAG AAA standards for reading and AA for graphical interactive targets.

### Role Definitions
- **Primary (`#2D4A3E` - Deep Forest Olive):** Used for primary buttons, focal navigation headers, active states, and reassurance certifications. It instills an immediate sense of reliability, longevity, and natural groundedness.
- **Secondary (`#C86446` - Terracotta Clay):** Used selectively for high-urgency conversion touchpoints: primary "Đặt Mua" / "Add to Cart" triggers, promotional tags, flash badges, and sale prices.
- **Tertiary (`#8A9A86` - Dried Sage):** Used for soft secondary verification indicators, gentle pill backgrounds, and ecological footprint meters.
- **Neutral (`#4A3B32` - Aged Oak Umber):** Replaces pure digital black for all titles, structural dividers, and high-priority body copy, avoiding harsh digital contrast on warm backgrounds.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#F9F6F0` (Warm Oatmeal)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` (Soft Cream White)
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Hover, Sub-sections):** `#F3ECE2` (Muted Sand)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E5DDD0` (Raw Linen Line)
- **Muted Body Text:** `#736357` (Weathered Bark)

## Typography

The pairing marries the literary charm of `Newsreader` (a transitional, warm editorial serif) with the legibility and friendly geometric clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Hierarchy Rules
- **Editorial Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Exclusively utilized for storytelling, collection titles, product display names, and section headlines. Set with a slightly tighter letter-spacing to impart craft and confidence.
- **Product Details & System Elements (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers all numeric data (dimensions, prices, condition percent), forms, navigational anchors, and transactional elements.
- **Numerics & Pricing:** Product pricing uses `Plus Jakarta Sans` at Semi-Bold or Bold weights, ensuring clear readability across small mobile product cards. Currency symbols (`₫`) sit with regular weight to keep numeric focus pristine.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid layout anchors wide visual negative space, mimicking an airy home lifestyle showroom.

### Breakpoints & Grid Composition
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, centered with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Product grids display 3 or 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters. Product listings display in 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Product listings default to a 2-column compact view or a single full-bleed hero card flow.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical pacing utilizes multiples of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Internal card padding stays compact (`1rem` on mobile, `1.25rem` on desktop) to prioritize item visual proofs.
- Generous outer section margins (`2.5rem` to `4rem`) give distinct vintage categories their own curated vignette feeling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm low-contrast outlines coupled with warm ambient shadows, avoiding sterile grey drops.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Tonal Amber Base:** All shadows use an alpha blend tinted with Umber (`rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.05)` to `rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.12)`) rather than neutral black, preserving the soft afternoon-light ambiance.
- **Level 0 (Flat / Embedded):** No shadow. Demarcated by a 1px solid `#E5DDD0` border. Applied to in-page specs, attributes, and input fields.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.03);` border: 1px solid `#E5DDD0`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Floating Cards):** `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.04);` translateY: -2px.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Cart Drawer / Sticky Menus):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.14);` backdrop-filter: `blur(8px)` with background `#F9F6F0` at 92% opacity.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a softened, tactile geometry that reflects natural pottery, turned wood, and hand-finished furniture.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Input controls, condition pill badges, small thumbnail frames, and inline action buttons.
- **Containers & Product Cards (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl` / 16px to 24px):** Product gallery photos, product display cards, testimonial units, and filter drawers.
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Condition labels ("Độ mới 95%"), curation tags ("Đã test kỹ"), category chips, and floating cart badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Thêm Vào Giỏ" / "Mua Ngay"):** Background `#C86446` (Terracotta), text `#FFFFFF`, rounded 8px (`rounded-md`), semi-bold. Hover: Darkened Terracotta (`#B35539`) with slight warm lift. Active: Inset press.
- **Secondary ("Xem Chi Tiết"):** Background `#2D4A3E` (Forest Olive), text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#22382F`.
- **Tertiary / Outline ("Lưu Yêu Thích"):** Background transparent, border 1px solid `#E5DDD0`, text `#4A3B32`. Hover: Background `#F3ECE2`, border `#D4C9B8`.

### Condition & Authenticity Badges
- **Condition Grade ("Độ mới 95%", "Nguyên bản 100%"):** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 10px`. Background `#F3ECE2`, border 1px solid `#E5DDD0`, text `#2D4A3E`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Technical Verification ("Đã test kỹ", "Đã khử khuẩn"):** Background `#2D4A3E` with text `#FFFFFF` or pale sage `#EAF0E9` with deep forest text, coupled with a small verification check icon.
- **Rarity / Discount ("Độc bản", "-20%"):** Background `#C86446`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Surface white (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border `#E5DDD0`, corner radius `16px`, subtle ambient shadow.
- **Media Container:** Aspect ratio 4:5 or 1:1, soft beige placeholder `#F3ECE2`, overflow hidden with image zoom on hover. Condition badges anchor to the top-left over the image with a subtle frosted backing.
- **Content Area:** Padding `16px`. Title in `title-sm` (`#4A3B32`), condition summary line, and price row with Terracotta accent.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E5DDD0`, corner radius 8px, text `#4A3B32`. Placeholder in `#A6988D`.
- **Focused State:** Border 1.5px solid `#2D4A3E`, subtle forest halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 74, 62, 0.12)`), no black outlines.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Filter pills allow rapid sorting by Room (Phòng Khách, Gian Bếp, Bàn Làm Việc) and Condition (99% Như Mới, 90-95%, Cổ Điển / Vintage).
- **Unselected:** Background `#F9F6F0`, border 1px solid `#E5DDD0`, text `#736357`.
- **Selected:** Background `#2D4A3E`, border 1px solid `#2D4A3E`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Selection Controls (Checkbox & Radio)
- Checkboxes feature an 8px radius with a deep forest olive fill `#2D4A3E` and white tick mark upon selection. Radios feature concentric forest rings over a clean cream base.

### Specialized Component: Condition Inspector Drawer
- Secondhand commerce requires transparency: clicking any condition badge triggers a slide-out inspect drawer with macro photography showcasing minor scuffs, patina details, and original markings, backed by clear restoration notes.
